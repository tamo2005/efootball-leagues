# Resend delivery research

- Official pricing: https://resend.com/pricing
- Official transactional email documentation: https://resend.com/docs/email-types
- The official pricing page currently lists the Transactional Email Free plan at $0/month with 3,000 emails/month and a 100-email-per-day limit.
- Resend describes transactional email as personalized, event-driven 1-to-1 communication, including account notifications; it supports application delivery through the REST API, SDKs, CLI, or SMTP.
- The eLeague notification use case is low-volume personalized matchday mail, so the free transactional tier is appropriate once the sender address is accepted by Resend.
